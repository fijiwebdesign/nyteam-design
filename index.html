About the Studio

Built in New York,
for New York.

We started NY Team because the best companies in the city deserve design that matches their ambition.

New York runs on two things: finance and media. We speak both languages fluently — from pitch decks that close Series B rounds to editorial systems that define how millions read the news.

Our studio sits at the intersection of Flatiron, SoHo, and Dumbo — three neighborhoods that shaped our understanding of what design can do for a business that takes itself seriously.
